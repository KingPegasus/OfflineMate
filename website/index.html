The private AI assistant that runs offline on your phone

OfflineMate runs language models on your phone, so your chats, notes, and reminders work without a cloud service or an account. Download a model once, then keep using it in airplane mode.

Available now for Android 7.0 and later, on phones and tablets. iOS is coming soon.

Features

An offline AI assistant that can do more than chat

Inference and personal context stay on your device. You choose a model tier at setup, and if a model fails to load, the app can fall back to a lighter tier.

💬

Offline AI chat in three tiers

Lite, Standard, and Full use progressively larger quantized models so you can balance speed and quality. Replies stream in, and optional “thinking” text helps you follow reasoning before the final answer.

Example “Explain recursion like I’m new to code” (works offline after the model is downloaded).

🧠

Private memory for your notes

On Standard and Full, questions can trigger local embedding and vector search (RAG) over your indexed notes. Lite skips retrieval for speed.

Example “What did I save about the dentist?”

⚡

Calendar, reminders, and alarms

A validated planner can run allow-listed tools for your calendar, reminders, alarms, contacts, and notes, only with the permissions you grant.

Examples “Remind me in 20 minutes to stretch”; “What’s on my calendar tomorrow?”

🎙

Voice input on your device

Push-to-talk speech recognition uses Whisper on your phone, then follows the same path as typed text. Replies can be read aloud with your device’s text-to-speech voices.

Example “Add a note to pick up bread” after you release the mic button.

🌐

Web search you can turn off

For questions that need current information, OfflineMate can fetch DuckDuckGo-backed results. It doesn’t run on every message. It’s on by default, and one setting turns it off.

Example “Search for population of Tokyo”

✦

Fast intent routing

Keyword routing steers reminders, memory lookup, or general chat without an extra router model, which saves latency and battery on small devices.

Example “Remind me…” is steered toward reminders early.

🗂

Multiple chats, kept only if you choose

Keep separate conversations, reopen any previous thread, and delete chats when you want. Chat retention is optional in settings and defaults to off.

Examples “Open chats”; “Delete this conversation”; “Keep chat history on device.”

How it works

From install to airplane mode in three steps

  1. Choose a tier for your phone

    Pick Lite, Standard, or Full based on your phone’s memory, then choose a model for that tier.

  2. Download the model once

    The chat, speech, and search files download to your phone. This is the step that needs a connection.

  3. Use it anywhere

    Chat, search your notes, and set reminders with or without a signal. Your phone does the processing.

FAQ

Common questions about offline AI on your phone

Does OfflineMate really work without internet?

Yes, once a model is downloaded. Chat, notes search and device tools run on your phone, so they keep working in airplane mode. You need a connection for the first model download and for web search.

Are my chats sent to a server?

No. OfflineMate has no cloud service that stores your chats, and responses are generated on your device. When web search runs, the search query goes to the search provider. Web search is on by default and can be turned off in Settings. Read the privacy summary.

Is OfflineMate free?

Yes. OfflineMate is free and open source under the MIT license. It needs no account and no subscription.

Which Android phones can run it?

OfflineMate supports Android 7.0 and later. The model tier you can use depends on memory: Lite targets 3 to 4 GB of RAM, Standard targets 6 to 8 GB, and Full targets 10 to 12 GB or more.

How much storage does it need?

The model download is about 430 MB to 1.3 GB for Lite, about 2.5 GB for Standard, and about 2.6 GB for Full.

Which AI models does it use?

The defaults are SmolLM2 135M on Lite, Gemma 4 E2B on Standard, and Qwen 3 4B on Full. Each tier also offers alternatives, including Llama 3.2, LFM 2.5, MiniCPM5 and Qwen 3.5. Compare all models.

Is it as capable as a cloud AI assistant?

No. Models small enough for a phone are less capable than large cloud models and can make mistakes, so check anything important. In exchange, your conversations stay on your device and work without a connection.

Is OfflineMate available on iPhone?

Coming soon. OfflineMate runs on Android today, and an iOS version for iPhone is on the way.
