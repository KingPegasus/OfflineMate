The private AI assistant that runs offline on your phone

OfflineMate runs language models on your phone, so your chats, notes, and reminders work without a cloud service or an account. Download a model once, then keep using it in airplane mode.

Available now for Android 7.0 and later, on phones and tablets. iOS is coming soon.

Features

An offline AI assistant that can do more than chat

Inference and personal context stay on your device. You choose a model tier at setup, and if a model fails to load, the app can fall back to a lighter tier.

💬

Offline AI chat in three sizes

Light, Balanced, and Smartest use progressively larger quantized models so you can balance speed and quality. Replies stream in, and optional “thinking” text helps you follow reasoning before the final answer.

Example “Explain recursion like I’m new to code” (works offline after the model is downloaded).

🖼

Ask about a photo

Pick a picture from your gallery and ask about it. A vision model (LFM 2.5 VL or Gemma 4 multimodal) reads it on your phone. No photo permission, and the image never leaves the device.

Examples “What does this sign say?”; “What flowers are these?”

🧠

Private memory for your notes

On Balanced and Smartest, questions can trigger local embedding and vector search (RAG) over your notes. Light matches keywords in your notes instead.

Example “What did I save about the dentist?”

⚡

Calendar, reminders, and alarms

A validated planner can run allow-listed tools for your calendar, reminders, alarms, contacts, and notes, only with the permissions you grant.

Examples “Remind me in 20 minutes to stretch”; “What’s on my calendar tomorrow?”

🎙

Voice input on your device

Push-to-talk speech recognition uses Whisper on your phone, then follows the same path as typed text. Replies can be read aloud with your device’s text-to-speech voices.

Example “Add a note to pick up bread” after you release the mic button.

🌐

Web search you can turn off

For questions that need current information, OfflineMate can fetch DuckDuckGo-backed results, and weather from Open-Meteo (only the place name is sent). It doesn’t run on every message. It’s on by default, and one setting turns it off.

Example “Search for population of Tokyo”

✦

Fast intent routing

Keyword routing steers reminders, memory lookup, or general chat without an extra router model, which saves latency and battery on small devices.

Example “Remind me…” is steered toward reminders early.

🗂

Multiple chats, kept only if you choose

Keep separate conversations, reopen any previous thread, and delete chats when you want. Chat retention is optional in settings and defaults to off.

Examples “Open chats”; “Delete this conversation”; “Keep chat history on device.”

Ask about any photo, offline

Tap the image button, pick a photo, and ask. Android’s photo picker hands over only the photo you choose, so OfflineMate needs no photo permission, and the copy it keeps is shrunk and stripped of location data.

  • “Which drink is cheapest?” reads the prices on a menu
  • “What flowers are these?” describes what’s in it
  • Send with no text for a description
  • Look for the image icon in Settings: LFM 2.5 VL 450M (Light), LFM 2.5 VL 1.6B (Balanced and Smartest), Gemma 4 E2B multimodal (Smartest)

One chat, four tools

A real conversation with the Balanced assistant (LFM 2.5 VL 1.6B), in airplane mode, with no account and no cloud. Each request goes to the right tool on the phone, and the reply comes back in seconds.

  • “Remind me in 20 minutes to stretch” sets a reminder
  • “What’s on my calendar tomorrow?” reads your calendar
  • “Add a note to pick up bread” saves a note
  • “What did I save about the dentist?” searches your notes

How it works

From install to airplane mode in three steps

  1. Choose a size for your phone

    Setup recommends Light, Balanced, or Smartest from your phone’s memory (RAM). Keep it, or pick another size or model.

  2. Download the model once

    The chat, speech, and search files download to your phone. This is the step that needs a connection. OfflineMate checks your free storage, asks before using mobile data, and shows the size, speed and time left, with a Cancel button.

  3. Use it anywhere

    Chat, search your notes, and set reminders with or without a signal. Your phone does the processing.

Why is a 1 GB model in Balanced, not Light?

Download size doesn’t decide how hard a model is for your phone to run. The number in its name does. That number, like the “1B” or “1.7B”, is how many parameters (billions of small values) the model has. Models are compressed so a 1.7B model downloads at about 1 GB. Your phone still works through every one of those 1.7 billion values for each word it writes, which is many times more work than a Light model.

On a phone with 3–4 GB of RAM, Android and your other apps already use most of the memory. A 1–2B model there would answer very slowly or be closed by the system. Phones with 6 GB or more run these models smoothly. So the tiers follow your phone’s RAM, not the download size.

FAQ

Common questions about offline AI on your phone

Does OfflineMate really work without internet?

Yes, once a model is downloaded. Chat, notes search and device tools run on your phone, so they keep working in airplane mode. You need a connection for the first model download and for web search.

Are my chats sent to a server?

No. OfflineMate has no cloud service that stores your chats, and responses are generated on your device. When web search runs, the search query goes to the search provider. Web search is on by default and can be turned off in Settings. Read the privacy summary.

Is OfflineMate free?

Yes. OfflineMate is free and open source under the MIT license. It needs no account and no subscription.

Which Android phones can run it?

OfflineMate supports Android 7.0 and later. The assistant size you can use depends on memory: Light targets 3 to 4 GB of RAM, Balanced targets 6 to 8 GB, and Smartest targets 10 GB or more.

How much storage does it need?

The default models download about 620 MB on Light and 2.3 GB on Balanced and Smartest. Alternatives range from 430 MB to 1.3 GB on Light, and up to 2.9 GB on Balanced and 4.2 GB on Smartest. OfflineMate checks your free storage before it starts.

Which AI models does it use?

The defaults also read photos: LFM 2.5 VL 450M on Light and LFM 2.5 VL 1.6B on Balanced and Smartest. Each size also offers alternatives, including Gemma 4, Qwen 3, Llama 3.2, LFM 2.5, MiniCPM5 and Qwen 3.5. Compare all models.

Can OfflineMate understand photos?

Yes, with a vision model: LFM 2.5 VL 450M on Light, LFM 2.5 VL 1.6B on Balanced and Smartest, or Gemma 4 E2B multimodal on Smartest. Pick a photo from your gallery and ask about it. The image is read on your phone and never uploaded.

Does OfflineMate need access to all my photos?

No. It opens Android’s photo picker, so you choose one photo at a time and the app only gets that one. It asks for no photo or storage permission. The copy it keeps is shrunk and has its location data removed.

Is it as capable as a cloud AI assistant?

No. Models small enough for a phone are less capable than large cloud models and can make mistakes, so check anything important. In exchange, your conversations stay on your device and work without a connection.

Is OfflineMate available on iPhone?

Coming soon. OfflineMate runs on Android today, and an iOS version for iPhone is on the way.
